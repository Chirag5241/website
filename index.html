I study the geometric structure of “intelligence”. Currently my work is on AI interpretability, specifically studying the geometry of how models internally represent a task.

I am an MSCS student at UIUC working on computational mechanics and decision theory, to better understand how a model represents and builds its understanding of the world. I am deeply inspired by the work on Why Nations Fail, and my current hypothesis focuses on seeing how AI models can better capture the structures of nations and institutions.

Research

My current research, begun at Simplex AI Safety, is on how LLMs form and update representations of latent belief states while making predictions. My goal at school is to show that models can learn and perform inference over structures identified by economics, game theory, political science, and complex-systems research.
